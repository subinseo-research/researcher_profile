MindFlex AI
People use generative AI for a wide range of purposes, yet general-purpose interfaces offer little support for matching tools to needs. This project designs a system that (1) supports intuitive model selection, (2) helps users articulate their information needs through guided dialogue, and (3) provides accurate citations, with the overarching goal of mitigating over-reliance on AI.
